## RWB App overview: what is the RWB app, what can it do, main screens and tabs
k: features, sections, layout, where is, menu, bottom bar, navigation
b: app
The RWB App is the free app of Ronin Warriors of Bengal (on Google Play in India; iPhone riders use it in Safari at roninwb.in/app and can "Add to Home Screen").
Bottom bar: Home, Service, Mileage, Track (the big orange middle button), MotoMart, Warriors, Events.
Top right: calendar, ? (Guides), bell (news and alerts) and your photo (Me).
Pookie (the AI buddy) is the orange helmet button. Most of the app works offline and syncs when you're back online.

## Can live location be shared only with family members? Track privacy: who can see my location
k: share location only family, private location, hide location from members, only my family see me, family only tracking, who sees me on track, location privacy, live location family
b: app
No. In the RWB App, Track has no "family only" option: while you share, every RWB member on the app can see you on the Track map, and members get a notice that you started riding.
Your RWB FAM (up to 3 family members you invite from Home → FAM) also see you live while you share. RWB FAM only ever see their own rider (plus riders on the same event ride), never the whole map.
If you want ONLY family to see you, don't share on Track; use WhatsApp or Google Maps live location with them instead. Stop sharing on Track any time: Track → Sharing live → Stop sharing.

## Track: how to share live location on a ride (Share, View, SOS)
k: start sharing, live location, track button, go live, share ride, how long, until I stop
b: app
Tap the orange Track button in the middle of the bottom bar. You get three choices: Share, View and SOS.
Share: pick how long (1 hour, 4 hours, 8 hours or "Until I stop"). You can add a short note (up to 60 letters, e.g. "towards Simlipal!") that shows next to your name. If an RWB event ride is on, pick it under "Riding an RWB event?" (the app picks it for you if you're Going).
In the Android app sharing keeps running with the screen locked (a small notification shows). In a browser, keep the app open on the phone mount; locking the phone pauses sharing.
To stop: Track → Sharing live → Stop sharing.

## Track map: seeing riders, speed, follow a rider, directions, map styles
k: view riders, map, where is everyone, rider speed, km/h, average speed, top speed, follow, directions, satellite, map style, full screen
b: app
Track → View shows everyone sharing right now. Pin ring colours: green = moving, amber = stopped, grey = no signal; the arrow shows direction and km/h shows on moving riders.
Tap a rider for: km/h now, average and top speed, km ridden this ride, how long they've shared, distance from you, Call, Directions (Google Maps) and Follow (the map keeps them in view).
Buttons on the map: full screen, map style (Street, Clean, Satellite), zoom, show everyone, and centre on me (tap again to follow yourself).
An event link (from the event page) highlights only that ride; other riders are dimmed under "Also riding now".

## Track ride lines: recent rides, ride replay, delete my ride line
k: ride history, route replay, playback, recent rides, past ride, ride path, line, delete ride
b: app
After a ride shared on Track (1 km or more), a line of the route appears under "Recent rides" on the Track screen for 7 days, grouped by day and by event. Tap it to replay the ride on a map (play/pause, slider, speed up to 600×; event rides play every rider together).
Privacy: the part within about 300 m of the start and end of every line is never shown, so nobody sees where you live. Members see every ride; RWB FAM (family) can play back and save their own rider's rides and the event rides their rider was on, without top speed. You (or an admin) can delete your own line any time; lines are deleted automatically 7 days after the ride.

## Save a ride to my phone, keep a ride forever, top speed of a ride
k: save ride, download ride, keep ride, saved rides, offline ride, ride gone after 7 days, top speed, highest speed, max speed, speed wrong, speedometer
b: app
In a ride's playback, tap the download button at the top right ("Save to phone"). The ride is kept inside the RWB app on your phone only (not on RWB's server), stays after the 7 days and plays even without internet. Saved rides are listed under "Saved on this phone" on the Track screen; tap the orange tick in playback to remove one. It stays on that phone only, so it's gone if you uninstall the app or clear its data.
Playback and Recent rides show each ride's top speed ("top km/h"). Top speed comes from the phone's GPS, which is the true speed; a bike's speedometer reads about 5–8% higher by law (it may never show less than the real speed), so 120 km/h on GPS is about 128 on the bike's speedometer. The phone sends its speed about every 5 seconds, so a very short burst can be missed.

## SOS in the RWB app: how to send an emergency alert
k: emergency, accident, help, breakdown alert, danger, sos button, cancel sos, i'm safe
b: app
Track → SOS → press and HOLD the button for 2 seconds. Every RWB admin and coordinator gets an urgent alert with your location (it's hold-to-send so it can't go by accident).
The SOS screen also lists the RWB team with Call buttons. In real danger also call 112.
When you're OK, open SOS again and tap "Cancel SOS · I'm safe". Admins can mark it resolved. RWB FAM members can't send SOS.

## Track notifications: alert when a rider starts sharing
k: notification track, riding live alert, turn off track alerts
b: app
When a rider starts a new ride on Track, members get "📍 <name> is riding live" (or "📍 <event> is live on Track" for an event ride, once per event every 3 hours). You never get your own.
Don't want these? Me → Notifications → turn off "Riders who start sharing on Track".

## The Track button spinning green ring
k: green circle, track tab glowing, spinning ring, live indicator
b: app
The green ring spinning around the middle Track button means someone in RWB is sharing live on Track right now. Tap Track → View to see who.

## Track battery and background tips (Android)
k: battery drain, location stops, sharing paused, background location, phone locked, battery optimization
b: app
In the RWB Android app, Track keeps sharing with the screen locked; allow location "all the time" if Android asks, and don't force-close the app. If sharing stops on its own, check that battery saver isn't restricting the RWB app (Settings → Apps → RWB → Battery → Unrestricted). In a browser, sharing pauses whenever you lock the phone or switch apps. For long rides keep the phone on a charger.

## RWB FAM: family members in the RWB app (invite, what they see, remove)
k: family, add family, wife, husband, parents, mother, father, son, daughter, brother, sister, girlfriend, invite code, fam code, family circle, fam app
b: app
RWB FAM lets you add up to 3 family members. Home → FAM shows your invite code (like RWB-XXXX) with WhatsApp, SMS and Copy buttons.
They open the link (Android gets the app, iPhone uses the web app), sign in with ANY Google account (they must remember which one) and enter your code.
They see: your live location when you share on Track (and riders on the same event ride), your odometer, RWB events, important announcements, members, the gallery and the VRN check.
They can't see expenses, RSVPs, MotoMart, Service, Mileage or anyone's phone number, and can't share location or send SOS.
Remove someone any time from Home → FAM → Remove (they lose access at once). Family members can also switch RWB FAM off in their own Me screen.

## How to update my odometer km in the app
k: odometer, change km, wrong km, update kilometers, reading
b: app
Tap the big kilometre number (the dial) on Home, type what your bike's odometer shows and tap Save. Adding a fill-up or a service with the odometer also updates it.

## My Ride: tracking parts wear (oil, chain, tyres, brake pads) and accessories
k: parts tracker, wear parts, ride health, replace part, chain kit, brake pads due, my ride name
b: app
Home → My Ride lists wear parts: engine oil, oil filter, chain lube, tyres, brake pads, chain & sprockets, air filter, spark plug, brake fluid, battery, cables, clutch plates and fork oil.
Tap a part to set its brand, size, date and km fitted and expected life. Life figures come from the TVS Ronin manual where it gives one, else typical figures (you can change them). A bar shows how much life is used; red = past its life, "Nearly due" = soon.
When you change a part, open it → "I replaced it: start a new one" (the old one stays in history).
Accessories & mods: add crash guard, top rack, exhaust, aux lights etc. with cost. Rename your ride (e.g. "Black Pearl"); only you see the name.

## Service screen: how to log a service and bill
k: add service, service history, service record, bill photo, service centre, free service, paid service, AMC, edit service, delete service
b: app
Tap Service at the bottom → "+ Add service". Date and km are filled in. Choose the type (Free, Paid, AMC, Repair, Self/DIY), pick or type the service centre, add what was done and the total cost, and attach the bill (photo, PDF or Word). Tap Save service.
Tap any service later to see it, view the bill, Edit or Delete. The card at the top shows when the next service is due.

## Next service due date: how the app works it out
k: service reminder, next service, service due, when is my service
b: app
The app follows the TVS Ronin service chart: 1st free at 750–1,000 km (or 30–45 days), 2nd free at about 6,000 km (6 months), 3rd free at about 12,000 km (12 months), then every 6,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first. Log your last service so the dates are exact. The Calendar and a reminder notification also show it.

## Mileage screen: how to add fuel and how mileage is calculated
k: add fuel, fill up, petrol log, kmpl, fuel log, mileage calculation, km per litre, full tank, DTE, range left, trip A, check badge, wrong mileage, unrealistic mileage
b: app
After EVERY fill (any amount, full tank or not) tap "+ Add fuel": litres, ₹ paid, odometer and date. Optional: "DTE before filling (km)" from your dash, "Trip A (km)" (reset it at every fill-up for the most exact distance) and "Full tank" if you filled till the nozzle clicked off. Trip A gives the exact distance; Full tank or DTE tell the app how much petrol you used.
The app follows the petrol in your tank: mileage = km since your last reading ÷ petrol used. With Full tank or DTE it knows the level (Ronin: fuel left ≈ 2 L + DTE ÷ the km per litre your dash uses, which the app learns), so even a part fill gets its own mileage at once (≈ when DTE is used) and counts in your average.
Without DTE/Full tank, a fill-up's mileage = km since your previous fill-up ÷ its litres; a part fill shows "Pending" and is joined with the next fill-up.
"Full tank" on a fill that can't reach the top makes the app ask "Full tank?" first (Yes / No, it was a part fill); older ones like that show "Check" until you open and save them. If Trip A and the odometer disagree, the app asks "Check the odometer"; when the odometer gives an impossible mileage but Trip A doesn't, Trip A is used (and the fill-up shows Check so you can fix the odometer).
Works for every bike in Me → My bikes: the app uses that bike's full tank capacity.
Fill-ups that don't add up (an odometer typo, a missed fill-up, more litres than the tank holds, mileage a bike can't do: a Ronin over 55 or under 25 km/l; over 50 counts only when 3 readings in a row are over 50) show "Check" and are left out until fixed.
Your average and the Mileage Warriorboard use the same readings and need at least 200 km of good readings. Typed something wrong? Tap the fill-up and fix it. Forgot to log a fill-up? Tap the fill-up marked Check or Pending → "+ Add a missed fill-up before this one", enter its litres and odometer, and the mileage sorts itself out.

## Mileage with more than one bike, and tank capacity
k: second bike, multiple bikes, other bike mileage, tank capacity, non ronin mileage
b: app
With 2 or 3 bikes, pick the bike when adding fuel; each bike has its own mileage (chips at the top of Mileage). Only the main bike's fill-ups count as odometer readings and for the Mileage Warriorboard.
For non-Ronin bikes the app needs the full tank capacity: Me → My bikes → the bike → Full tank capacity. The Ronin's 14 L is known already.

## Events: RWB rides, joining a ride, I'm going, guests
k: event, ride plan, rsvp, going, join ride, not going, guest, plus one, meetup, breakfast ride

Tap "I'm going 🏍️" to join (everyone can see you joined); "Not going" takes you off. If the team allows it, switch on "Bringing a guest (+1)". If slots are full you'll see "Event full".

## Event expenses: split costs with the group

On an event page, the Expenses section lets people who are going add shared costs (e.g. breakfast ₹1,200). The app splits each expense only among those going and shows who pays whom. You must join the event to see and add expenses.

## Creating and managing events (admins and coordinators)

b: app
Admins and coordinators see "+ Create event" in Events (every member gets a notification). They can add a landscape cover photo, slots, guests on/off, a call link and a reminder.

## Warriors and Warriorboards (leaderboards)
k: leaderboard, ranking, top riders, rank, warriorboard, hall of fame, track warriors, mileage warriors, road warriors, service warriors, pookie warriors

Tap Warriors at the bottom. Five boards: Track Warriors (km ridden while sharing on Track), Mileage Warriors (best average km/l of each rider's main bike), Service Warriors (services logged), Road Warriors (odometer km) and Pookie Warriors (XP from Teach Pookie).
Switch period with Week, Month, 3M, 6M, Year, All. Swipe or tap tiles to change board; your own rank shows on each tile.

## MotoMart: buy and sell bike gear, accessories and RWB merch
k: motomart, buy, sell, marketplace, used parts, pre-owned, second hand gear, shop
b: app
MotoMart (bottom bar) has tabs All, New, Pre-owned and RWB Merch, round category buttons, search (top right) and sort.

## How to sell something on MotoMart
k: sell gear, post item, list item, sell helmet, sell accessory, mark sold, delete post
b: app
Tap "Sell gear" on Home or "+ Sell something" in MotoMart. Add clear photos, the name, category, condition and price, then "Send for approval". An admin or coordinator checks it (usually within a day); an admin's own post goes live at once.
Limits: 8 active posts and 5 new posts a day. When it's sold, mark it sold (it greys out and is deleted 15 days later). WhatsApp shares of a post hide your name and number; buyers contact you from inside the app.

## Guides: maintenance PDFs, riding tips and app how-to videos

b: app
Tap the ? at the top. Three categories: App (short how-to videos for the app), Maintenance (bike care PDFs, videos and pictures; opens first) and Ride (riding tips). Filters: All, Videos, PDFs, Pictures, plus search. Guides open inside the app.

## Pookie: the RWB AI, daily question limit, photos, languages
k: pookie, ai, chatbot, ask pookie, questions left, limit, new chat, bengali, hindi, photo question
b: app
Tap the orange Pookie button. Ask about Ronin care, gear, routes, RWB or the app, and keep the chat going with follow-ups. Pookie understands English, Bengali, Hindi, Banglish and Hinglish. You can attach a photo (📷).
Each member gets 20 questions a day ("questions left today" shows under the box); questions Pookie can't answer don't count. The chat is kept only on your phone; "New chat" clears it.
Pookie answers from what RWB warriors and admins taught it plus RWB's checked notes. If it doesn't know, the question goes to Teach Pookie as URGENT.

## Teach Pookie: answer questions and earn XP
k: teach pookie, xp, levels, road guru, ronin sensei, urgent question, earn points
b: app
Home → Explore → Teach Pookie (or the chip in Pookie). Answer Pookie's questions from your own experience: brands, km, prices, steps, what worked. Admins or coordinators check each answer; you get +10 XP for every approved answer (2 XP while it waits). Admin answers go live at once.
URGENT questions are ones riders asked Pookie that it couldn't answer, so they help right away. Skip any you don't know. Top teachers show on the Pookie Warriorboard.

## Joining RWB: how to become a member, join form, approval
k: join rwb, membership, how to join, register, application, apply, add warriors, new member, waiting approval
b: app
New riders join in the RWB App or at roninwb.in/join: sign in with Google and fill the short join form. An admin checks it, usually within a day. Your status screen shows Request sent, On hold (with a note) or Not approved (with a reason).
Every rider is welcome: Ronin owners, Ronin booked, planning to buy, or riding another bike. Questions before joining go to RWB's Instagram DM, never admins' numbers.
Riders without a Ronin also tell the admins why they'd like to join (for example, they know someone in RWB, or it's a special request). Riders on another bike give that bike's VRN, which an admin checks, the same as for Ronin owners. If something is missing, the status screen says "One more step" with an "Add it now" button.

## Offline use: does the RWB app work without internet?
k: offline, no internet, no network, sync, changes waiting, hills no signal
b: app
Yes, mostly. Screens you've opened before show from the phone, and fuel logs, services, reminders, ride parts and event RSVPs saved offline are kept and sent automatically when you're back online (a pill shows "Offline" or "N changes waiting"). Things that need live data (Track, MotoMart posting, Pookie) need internet.

## How do I set a nickname? How do I change the name shown in the app?
k: nickname, display name, shown as, first name, change my name in the app, call me, name on track, name on warriorboard
b: app
Me → Name in the app → "Shown as". Pick First name (the default) or Nickname and type it (2 to 20 letters). Everyone shows by first name unless they pick a nickname. Your chosen name shows on Home, Warriorboards, events and Going lists, the members list and notifications. The Track map, ride replays and WhatsApp messages from the RWB team always use your real first name. To change your full name itself, use Me → Edit profile (an admin checks it first).
