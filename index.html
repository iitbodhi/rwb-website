The unscripted brotherhood of the Ronins

Ronin Warriors of Bengal is a riding community born from the open road and the spirit of Bengal. Our heart beats with the TVS Ronin 225, and riders of other machines ride with us too.

With 400+ Ronins, 20+ patron service centers and 15+ service camps, we don't just ride together. We grow together, with loyalty and freedom at the core.
