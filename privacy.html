Privacy Policy

For the RWB App (roninwb.in/app), roninwb.in, the RWB join form and RWB's member records. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Ronin Warriors of Bengal (“RWB”, “we”) is a free, non-profit, volunteer-run community of TVS Ronin owners. We respect your privacy and handle your personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and your choices. It is part of the RWB Terms & Conditions.

What we collect

  • When you join through the RWB join form (the only way to join): name, date of birth, WhatsApp number, an alternative number if you give one, email address, whether you own a Ronin, your bike's registration number (VRN), blood group, current locality, permanent hometown, an emergency contact number, your agreement to the Terms, and your answers to the form's other questions (such as stickers and social media).
  • When you sign in with Google: your name and email address, only to match you to your join-form entry (sign in with the same email you joined with). We never see your Google password.
  • What you add in the app: profile photo and nickname, up to three bikes, service records, bills and invoices, fuel fill-ups and odometer readings, the parts and accessories you track in My Ride (brands, dates, km and costs), answers you give in Teach Pookie, documents you save in My documents, reminders you add in the Calendar (what, when, how often and a note), Motomart posts, event responses and shared expenses, and your app settings (such as what you share with other members).
  • Track (only when you choose to share): your live location, speed and the time of each update, while you share. Kilometres ridden while sharing are added to your Track Warriors total. If you send an SOS, your location at that moment.
  • Track in the RWB Android app (location in the background): while you share, the app collects your location even when the app is closed or not in use (the screen off, the app minimised or swiped away), so your ride group, your RWB FAM and the RWB team (for SOS) can see where you are. It runs only while you share, shows a notification the whole time, and stops when you tap Stop or the time you chose runs out. It is never used for ads or sold.
  • Track note (optional): a short note you add when sharing (up to 60 characters, e.g. “towards Simlipal!”), shown next to your name while you share and deleted with your location.
  • RWB FAM (family of members): if a member invites you to RWB FAM, your name, your Google email, and, if you give them, a contact number and address. We also keep when you agreed to the Terms and whether you turned RWB FAM off.
  • Technical data: if you turn on notifications, a notification address for your device. Once a day, when you open the app, it notes which copy of the RWB App you use (the Play Store app, the app downloaded from roninwb.in, or a browser), its version number and the date, so admins can see who is on which copy and help members move to the Play Store app. The app itself does not track you or use advertising cookies.

Why we use it

  • To review and approve membership, check that each VRN belongs to one member, and keep non-members out of the app.
  • To contact you about RWB, rides, events and your membership, including reminders to complete your profile.
  • For rider safety: your blood group and emergency contact are kept only for emergencies on rides and events.
  • To run the app's features for you: your service history, mileage tracker, documents, calendar and reminders, Motomart, events and leaderboards. This includes sending you reminder notifications before your documents expire, your service or a part falls due, or a reminder you set.
  • Your agreement to the Terms is our basis for using your data. You can withdraw it at any time by leaving RWB (see “Your rights”).

Who can see it

  • Other members see only your name, nickname and photo. Your VRN and bike numbers, bike model, locality and average mileage stay hidden unless you choose to share them in the app (Me → Privacy). They never see your phone number, email, date of birth, blood group, hometown or emergency contact.
  • VRN check: a member who types a full vehicle number learns whether it belongs to an RWB member (“Yeah! RWB Member.” or “Naah.”). If it does, they also see the owner's name partly hidden (for example “Bod****** M***”) and the first part of the area they gave (for example “Salt Lake”). They see the full name only if the owner chose to share their VRN in Me → Privacy. Admins and coordinators see who it is, to help members.
  • Joining with a VRN that's already registered: the app shows a short hint (the first two letters of the email and a few digits of the WhatsApp number it's registered with), so a returning member can sign in with the right account.
  • Track: while you share your live location (you choose 1, 4 or 8 hours, or until you stop), every member on the app can see you on the Track map. Your WhatsApp number there is shown only to admins and coordinators. When you stop, or the time runs out, your location is deleted; only your Track Warriors kilometres and number of rides are kept. An SOS goes to admins and coordinators with your location and shows on the Track map until it's closed (at most 24 hours).
  • RWB FAM: a member can invite up to three family members. While RWB FAM is on, they see that member's odometer and live location when the member shares it on Track (and riders on the same event ride, without phone numbers), RWB events (without RSVPs or expenses), admin announcements, the member list as a member sees it, and the VRN check. The inviting member sees their FAM users' names, numbers and when they joined; RWB admins and coordinators see all FAM users' details. A FAM user can turn RWB FAM off at any time in Me, after which nothing is shown to them, and can alert the admins if they were added without consent. Removing a FAM user, or a member leaving RWB, ends their access.
  • Warriorboards (leaderboards): your name or nickname, photo, bike model, and your scores on each board: kilometres ridden while sharing on Track, odometer reading, average mileage, number of services logged, and Teach Pookie XP with the number of answers approved. Your rank on each board is shown to you and to other members. You can hide yourself from all of them in Me → Privacy.
  • RWB admins and coordinators can see members' details to manage membership, safety, birthdays and Motomart. Admins keep a private copy of the member list in RWB's Google Sheet, which only admins can open and which stays in step with the app.
  • Birthdays (day and month) are shown only to RWB admins and coordinators, for the club's birthday wishes.
  • The RWB team's (admins' and coordinators') names and phone numbers are shown to all members, so anyone can reach them in an emergency. Someone who signs in to the app with an email that isn't registered sees the admins' first names and WhatsApp numbers, so they can ask which email they joined with.
  • Finding your account: someone signed in with an unregistered email can enter a WhatsApp number and VRN that belong to the same member to see a hint of that member's email (e.g. ne•••@gmail.com). Adding the member's date of birth as well lets them use the Google account they're signed in with, which becomes the member's RWB email. If the membership was already used on the app with another Google account, that account loses access and RWB admins are notified. Tries are limited to 10 an hour.
  • Only you can see your service records, bills, fuel log, My Ride, your Calendar reminders and the documents in My documents (driving licence, RC, PUC, insurance and others). RWB admins cannot open them.
  • Calendar and reminders: only you see your calendar's personal dates: your reminders, document expiry dates, next service, My Ride parts due, and your own birthday and RWB anniversary. To send you reminder notifications, the app keeps your next service and part due dates and your reminders in the database, visible only to you; RWB admins cannot see them. You can turn these notifications off in Me → Notifications. RWB events and Indian holidays are the same for everyone. Admins and coordinators see members' birthdays (day and month only) for the coming month, as they already do.
  • Events: members see who responded to an event. Shared expenses are visible only to the people going and to the RWB team.
  • Motomart: what you post (photos, price, description) and your name and WhatsApp number on that post are visible to members, so buyers can contact you.

Services we use

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we show no ads. We use these services to run RWB, each only for its purpose:

  • Supabase stores the app's data, with access rules that enforce the limits above.
  • Google provides sign-in, the join form and RWB's member sheet.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and the app.
  • Your browser's notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver app notifications (and, in the Android app, Track's wake-up messages) if you turn them on.
  • Google Play delivers and updates the Android app if you install it from the Play Store.
  • Vehicle-registration lookup services (such as VAHAN, CarInfo or Motorpedia) may be used by admins to check that a VRN is valid. Only the VRN is entered.
  • Groq or Google Gemini answer questions asked to Pookie (see below).
  • Google's public “Holidays in India” calendar gives the Calendar its list of holidays. The list is fetched by RWB's server; nothing about you is sent. “Add to Google Calendar” opens Google Calendar with that one item filled in, only when you tap it.

Where your data is stored: the app's database is in India (Supabase, Mumbai). Some of the other services above, such as Google, GitHub and the AI services, may process data on servers outside India. Indian law allows this, and the same protections in this policy apply wherever your data is kept.

Pookie (the AI assistant)

Questions you ask Pookie, any photo you attach, your first name or nickname (so Pookie can greet you), and a short summary of your own bike data (bike model, odometer, last service, average mileage) are sent to an AI service, Groq or Google Gemini, to get an answer. These services may use it to improve their products. Pookie does not see other members' details. Please don't type personal details such as phone numbers or addresses into the chat. Your chat history stays on your phone.

Teach Pookie: answers you give in Teach Pookie are saved with your name so RWB admins can review them. An approved answer becomes part of what Pookie knows: it is added, with your first name only, to the background sent to the AI service with other members' questions, so Pookie can use it in its answers and say which RWB rider the tip came from. Your XP and number of approved answers appear on the Pookie Warriorboard. Don't include personal details in your answers. Admins may edit or reject answers, and you can ask an admin to remove yours.

Questions Pookie can't answer: Pookie only answers from what RWB riders and admins taught it. If it doesn't know, your question is saved as a general question (without your name) and shown to members in Teach Pookie marked URGENT, so someone can teach the answer. Don't put personal details in your questions.

How long we keep it

  • While you are a member, we keep your details so RWB can run.
  • If you leave or are removed, your app account and everything you added in the app (service records, bills, fuel log, My Ride, documents, calendar reminders) are deleted. A copy of your join-form entry is kept in the admins' private member sheet as a record of who was a member and why they left, so that removals can be enforced. You can ask us to delete it.
  • Join-form entries that are not approved are deleted from the admins' sheet straight away, and from the app's records after 60 days.
  • Entries replaced by a newer entry from the same person are kept in the same private sheet for record-keeping, and deleted on request.

Your rights

  • See and correct: view and update your details any time in the RWB App (Me). Changes to your name, number and VRN are checked by an admin.
  • Control sharing: choose what other members see in Me → Privacy, and hide yourself from the leaderboards.
  • Withdraw and erase: delete your account any time in the RWB App (Me → Delete my account), or by messaging an admin, and ask us to delete your data, including the copy in the admins' sheet. Steps: roninwb.in/delete-account.html.
  • Ask or complain: write to our Grievance Officer (below). We acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate someone: you can name a person to use these rights for you if you die or can't act yourself. Tell an admin or the Grievance Officer.
  • Your language: this policy is in English. Ask us and we'll explain it in Bengali or Hindi.

Keeping it safe

Access to member data is limited by role and protected by sign-in. Admins are asked to keep members' details confidential and use them only for RWB. No online service is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we will tell you without delay, with what happened, what it means for you and what you can do, and we will report it to the Data Protection Board of India and CERT-In within the times the law sets.

Children

RWB is for riders aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell members about important changes in the app or the WhatsApp groups.
