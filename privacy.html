Privacy Policy

For the RWB App (roninwb.in/app) and roninwb.in. Last updated 23 October 2026.

Ronin Warriors of Bengal (RWB) is a free, non-commercial riding community. The RWB App is free for everyone. This page explains what information we keep and why.

What we collect

  • From the RWB join form: name, date of birth, WhatsApp number, email, bike and registration number, blood group, locality, hometown and emergency contact.
  • When you sign in with Google: your name and email address, only to recognise you as a member. We never see your Google password.
  • What you add in the app: service records, bills or invoices you upload, and fuel fill-ups for the mileage tracker.

Why we use it

  • To let members into the app and keep non-members out.
  • To wish members on their birthdays (only the day and month are shown, never the year).
  • To show you your own service history and mileage.
  • Blood group and emergency contact are kept for rider safety on group rides.

Who can see it

  • Your service records, bills and fuel log are visible only to you. RWB admins cannot open them.
  • RWB admins can see the member details from the join form, to manage membership.
  • Other members see your name, nickname, photo, bike model, bike registration number and locality in the member list (so riders can check whether a Ronin they meet on the road is with RWB), your name on your birthday, and your latest odometer reading on the leaderboard (you can hide yourself from it in Me → Privacy). They never see your phone number, email, blood group or emergency contact, except that on your birthday members can add your WhatsApp number to their wish so WhatsApp tags you in the group.
  • The phone numbers of the RWB team (super admin, admins and Motomart coordinators) are shown to all members, so anyone can reach them in an emergency.
  • Motomart coordinators can see Motomart posts waiting for approval. They can't see member details.
  • We never sell or rent your information, and we show no ads. The only outside services that receive anything you write are the AI services that answer Pookie questions (see below).

Pookie (the AI chatbot)

Questions you ask Pookie, any photo you attach, and a short summary of your own bike data (bike model, odometer, last service, average mileage) are sent to an AI service, Groq or Google Gemini, to get an answer. These services may use it to improve their products. Don't type personal details such as phone numbers or addresses into the chat. Your chat history stays on your phone.

Where it is stored

App data is stored securely with Supabase (a database service), with access rules that enforce the limits above. Sign-in is handled by Google.

Deleting your data

If you leave RWB, an admin removes your profile, your app account and everything you added (service records, bills and fuel log). You can also ask any RWB admin on WhatsApp to delete your data at any time.

Contact

Questions? Message an RWB admin on WhatsApp or on Instagram, or email ronin.warriors.bengal@gmail.com.
