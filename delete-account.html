Delete your RWB App account

For the RWB App (Ronin Warriors of Bengal) on Android, iPhone and the web.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the RWB App and sign in.
  2. Tap Me (your photo, top right).
  3. Scroll to App and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm, and type DELETE.

RWB FAM users: the same steps, in Me → Delete my account.

Without the app

Email ronin.warriors.bengal@gmail.com from the email address you use for RWB, or message an RWB admin on WhatsApp, with the subject “Delete my RWB account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your RWB App sign-in, profile photo and nickname, bikes, service records, bills and invoices, fuel log and odometer readings, documents in My documents, event responses, shared expenses, Talk voice messages, notification settings and live location.
  • Motomart posts you made and your Track Warriors kilometres.
  • For RWB FAM users: your FAM access and sign-in.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A record that you were a member and when you left (your join-form entry), in the admins' private member list, so membership rules can be enforced. Ask us and we delete this too, within 30 days.
  • Voice messages already received by others are deleted after at most 7 days.
